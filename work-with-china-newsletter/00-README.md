## About the Author

Wenchao Wang helps global professionals succeed in Chinese companies. With 20 years of experience working across China, Singapore, Australia and beyond — visiting 2,000+ companies in 15+ countries — he founded Work With China to bridge the cross-cultural trust gap between Chinese companies going global and the international talent they need.

He is the author of **"Work With China: From Zero to Offer"** — a step-by-step playbook for working with Chinese companies.
